What is Atlas?

Atlas is an open-source LLM inference engine written in pure Rust and CUDA, with GPU kernels hand-tuned for NVIDIA DGX Spark. It runs open-weight models on your own hardware and serves them through an OpenAI-compatible API, with tool calling and streaming responses, so existing clients and agents can point at it unchanged. It is licensed AGPL-3.0, and its code, model recipes and benchmarks are public.

Which models and hardware does Atlas support?

Atlas runs open models from the Qwen, Gemma, Nemotron and Mistral families; the Atlas engine page lists the supported models and recipes. It is verified on NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10 Grace Blackwell), its kernels are also compiled for AMD Strix Halo, and it has been submitted to MLPerf Inference v6.1 on both.

How fast is Atlas?

Atlas publishes its benchmarks with the hardware and method named. As published on atlasinference.io on 17 November 2026, for unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4 on an NVIDIA GB10 (121.7 GB unified memory), 128 input and 1,024 output tokens, mean of 3 timed repetitions:

Concurrent requestsAtlasvLLM + MTP
123.59 tok/s19.72 tok/s
32291.01 tok/s283.48 tok/s
128478.11 tok/s358.57 tok/s

At 128 concurrent requests that is 1.333 times the throughput of the matched vLLM configuration. The full Atlas benchmark methodology and the raw benchmark results are public.
