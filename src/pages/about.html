About Avarok Cybersecurity

Avarok Cybersecurity builds post-quantum security and infrastructure software in Rust: the open-source Citadel Protocol SDK and the products built around it.

What does Avarok do?

Avarok develops post-quantum communication protocols and the software that uses them. The Citadel Protocol is the foundation: a Rust SDK for end-to-end encrypted client-server and peer-to-peer networking. Citadel Workspace, the Citadel Agent and synchronized.work are built on it; CertifiedCopy is another Avarok project; and Atlas Inference comes from Atlas Cybernetics, the AI-inference company founded by Avarok's founder.

Who founded Avarok?

Avarok was founded by Thomas Braun, a Rust engineer working in post-quantum cryptography and AI inference. As founder of Avarok Cybersecurity he leads the development of post-quantum communication protocols, created the Citadel Protocol, and provides Rust consulting for complex systems. His patent for the Recursive Cryptography Protocol, US patent 12224993, describes a post-quantum session-layer protocol with configurable security settings such as matrix-based ratcheting, enforced per-packet perfect forward secrecy and multi-layered encryption.

He also founded Atlas Cybernetics, which builds the Atlas Inference Engine: LLM inference in pure Rust and CUDA, hand-tuned for NVIDIA DGX Spark.
