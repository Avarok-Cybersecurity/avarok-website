What does CertifiedCopy do?

CertifiedCopy reads a Messages database in your browser and writes a PDF, CSV, JSON or RSMF of any conversation. The reading, the searching and the PDF are all done by your own computer: nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload it to, unless you ask its notary to witness a finished document, and then only that PDF is sent, once. It works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox, needs no account, and installs as an app that runs offline.

How do I export my iPhone messages to PDF?

  1. Choose a file: a chat.db from your Mac, a whole iPhone backup folder, or the sms.db inside a backup.
  2. Read the conversation: conversations on the left, messages on the right, as they looked on the phone, including tapbacks, replies, edits and attachments.
  3. Export it: one conversation or several, whole or between two dates, as a PDF transcript, CSV, JSON or RSMF.

What does every export record?

Each export opens with a record of the file it came from and that file's SHA-256, the messages included, the date range covered, when it was made, the document's own digest, and whether a notary witnessed it. The record is shown on screen before you produce the document.

What does CertifiedCopy cost?

Reading, copying and exporting are free. The optional notary, which witnesses a document and later verifies a witnessed copy, has a free tier and paid tiers listed on the CertifiedCopy pricing page. A Mac app, signed and notarised by Apple, can read messages from the Mac and from a connected iPhone.
